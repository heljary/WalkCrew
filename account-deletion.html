Google Play Data Safety & User Rights

WalkCrew Account & Data Deletion

In compliance with Google Play User Data policies, WalkCrew allows all users to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated fitness, social, and personal data.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can delete your account immediately inside the WalkCrew Android application:

  1. Open the WalkCrew app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account section.
  4. Tap Delete my account and all data.
  5. Confirm by tapping the red Permanently Delete button.

What Data is Permanently Deleted

  • Authentication Record: Your Firebase Auth credentials (UID, Google account link).
  • User Profile: Name, handle, display color index, daily step goal, notification settings, and FCM device tokens.
  • Step & Activity History: Daily step counts, weekly records, and streak logs.
  • Crew Memberships: Automatic removal from all crews you joined or created.
  • Social Interactions: Nudge records and reactions left on crew leaderboards.
  • Local Cache: DataStore keys, local step database, and cached preferences on your device.

* Data retention period: Deletion is processed immediately upon request and is non-reversible.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request Form

If you no longer have access to the app or device, submit your request below using the email linked to your WalkCrew account. Requests are processed within 48 hours.

WalkCrew adheres to strict zero-selling policies for personal and health data.
